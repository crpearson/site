# Apex Evo 5 + FETtec — a SoloGood dead end

This is not a build to copy. It is not a viable Lost Penny Apex. It is history.

I tried an Apex Evo reference stack on FETtec. The mounts I designed were tied to a SoloGood Apex Evo that already sat 2mm taller than the original ImpulseRC frame. SoloGood later stretched the thing another way for the DJI O4 Pro — about 4mm over original. Stupid changes. Myopic decision making. It caused me no end of design heartache.

Going forward, none of my work matches anything other than the original ImpulseRC Apex Evo hole spacing and vertical dimensions. I will drop models that follow SoloGood’s stretch.

The attempt still does not fly right (likely micro desyncs from motor wires run under the ESC). Glow-in-the-dark TPU looks cool and is likely to crack on impact — something I’ve learned along the way.

If you want the long bench story, it is on [RotorBuilds](https://rotorbuilds.com/build/36919/). This page exists so the site does not pretend that path was a success.

## What this is not

- Not a signature Apex to buy parts for.
- Not iteration-1 of a flying line.
- Not advice to run SoloGood’s revised dims.
